# Sales & Marketing Orchestration

## Operating rhythm
- **Shared source of truth:** target account flag, tier, owner, and stage live in the CRM, and both teams work from the same list.
- **Weekly account standup (30 minutes):** review engagement changes, new signals, next actions per Tier 1 account, and blockers.
- **Monthly program review:** pipeline and engagement by tier, which plays are working, and list changes.

## Engagement triggers for sales
Define what marketing signal prompts a sales action, for example: three or more people from the account engaging in 14 days, a pricing or comparison page visit from a target account, or a surge on a relevant intent topic. Route these to the account owner with context, and set an SLA for follow-up.

## Measurement
| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Coverage | Share of target accounts with known contacts for each buying role |
| Account engagement | Share of accounts showing meaningful engagement in the period |
| Opportunity creation rate | Target accounts that became opportunities |
| Pipeline and win rate vs control | Lift compared with similar non-target accounts |
| Deal size and cycle length | Whether ABM accounts close bigger and faster |

Give it at least two to three quarters before judging the program, since enterprise cycles are long.
