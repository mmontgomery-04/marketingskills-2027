# Tiers & Plays

## Tiers
| Tier | Typical size | Approach | Personalization |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 (strategic) | 10–50 accounts | Dedicated plan per account | Custom content, exec engagement, bespoke events |
| 1:few (cluster) | 50–500 accounts | Plans per cluster (industry, use case, competitor installed) | Content tailored to the cluster's shared pain |
| 1:many (programmatic) | 500–5,000 accounts | Automated, signal-driven | Industry and persona-level messaging |

Capacity check: a Tier 1 list should be small enough that each account owner can actively work every account each week.

## Plays by stage
| Stage | Goal | Example plays |
|---|---|---|
| Aware | Get known by the buying committee | Account-targeted LinkedIn ads and Thought Leader ads, industry-specific content, executive social engagement |
| Engaged | Start conversations | Personalized outbound sequences, invitations to roundtables or dinners, tailored assessments or benchmarks |
| Opportunity | Help the deal progress | Custom business-case content, reference calls, executive sponsor outreach, workshop or proof-of-concept offers |
| Customer | Expand | Adoption reviews, expansion plays to adjacent teams, advocacy asks |

## Example 1:few play: "Competitor installed"
Audience: accounts using a named competitor with a renewal window in the next six months. Assets: migration guide, comparison page, a case study from a customer who switched. Sequence: two weeks of targeted ads to the buying committee, then SDR outreach referencing the switching story, then an invitation to a migration workshop. Exit signal: meeting held or opportunity created.

## Direct mail and gifting
Use it for Tier 1 and selected Tier 2 accounts at a moment that matters (after a first meeting, before an exec briefing). Send something useful or relevant to the conversation, and always pair it with a personal follow-up.
