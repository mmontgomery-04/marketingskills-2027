# Trade Shows & Field Events

## Trade show
- **Before:** book meetings with target accounts 3–4 weeks out; promote booth activity; train staff on qualifying questions.
- **Booth:** one clear message, a demo station, a reason to stop (live teardown, assessment, quality giveaway); staff stand outside the booth, not behind tables.
- **Lead capture:** scan + 3 qualifying notes (role, pain, timing, rating A/B/C).
- **Side events:** a dinner or happy hour for Tier 1 accounts often beats the booth for pipeline.

## Executive dinner
12–20 guests, invite-only, peer-level, a topic (not a pitch), a customer or analyst as host, venue near the target audience. Personal invites from sales/execs; confirm 2–3 times; expect 20–30% no-shows.

## Budget lines
Sponsorship/booth · booth build and shipping · travel · swag · side events · promotion · lead retrieval · contingency (10–15%).

## Follow-up
A-leads: personal email + call within 24h. B: tailored sequence. C: nurture. Send everyone the promised resource.
