# Webinar Playbook

## Timeline
| When | Task |
|---|---|
| T–4 weeks | Topic, speakers (ideally a customer or creator, not just your team), title, landing page, Event schema |
| T–3 weeks | Email invite #1, social announcement, paid promotion starts |
| T–2 weeks | Email #2, speaker posts, partner promotion |
| T–1 week | Email #3 (non-registrants), reminder to registrants |
| T–1 day / T–1 hour | Reminders (calendar file + join link) |
| Live | Polls, Q&A, a clear next step offer |
| T+1 day | Recording + resources to all registrants; tailored email for attendees vs no-shows |
| T+1–2 weeks | Repurpose: blog recap, clips, LinkedIn carousel |

## Benchmarks (directional)
Registration-to-attendance ~35–45% for B2B webinars; on-demand views add significantly over time.

## Titles that register
Specific outcome + audience + format: "How 3 RevOps Leaders Cut Forecast Error in Half (Live Teardown)".
