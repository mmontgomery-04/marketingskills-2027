# B2B Marketing Site & SaaS Patterns

## Marketing site IA
Top nav: Product (or Platform) · Solutions (by role / industry / use case) · Pricing · Resources · Customers · Company · [Log in] · **[Primary CTA]**
- Mega menus for Product/Solutions with short descriptors under each link.
- Persistent primary CTA in the header; secondary CTA (e.g. "Take a tour") beside it.
- Footer as a sitemap; include security/trust, status page, and careers.

## Pricing page
3–4 plans, recommended plan highlighted, monthly/annual toggle, feature comparison table below, FAQ for billing/security objections, enterprise "Contact sales".

## SaaS onboarding
- Define the activation event (the "aha"); remove every step not needed to reach it.
- Checklists with progress, sample data, and empty states that teach.
- Delay non-essential setup (team invites, integrations) until after first value.

## Dashboards
Most important metric top-left; comparisons (vs last period/target); drill-down from summary to detail; consistent date controls.

## Tables and data-heavy UI
Sticky headers, sortable columns, filters with visible state, bulk actions, density toggle, and export.
