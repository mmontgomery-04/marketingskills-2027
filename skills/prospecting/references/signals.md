# Buying Signals

| Signal | Why it matters | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| Hiring for related roles | Budget and pain are active | LinkedIn Jobs, careers pages, Clay |
| New leader in buying role (first 90 days) | New leaders buy new tools | LinkedIn, press releases |
| Funding round | New budget, growth pressure | Crunchbase, PitchBook, news |
| Tech stack change | Integration or replacement window | BuiltWith, HG Insights, job descriptions |
| Third-party intent topics | Researching the category | Bombora, G2 Buyer Intent, 6sense |
| First-party intent | Visiting pricing/comparison pages | Website visitor ID (e.g. RB2B, Leadfeeder, Warmly, HubSpot Breeze Intelligence) |
| Competitor contract renewals | Switching window | Sales intel, reviews |
| Expansion / new office | New needs | News, LinkedIn |
| Engaged with our content/event | Warm | MAP, webinar platform |

Act on signals within days; their value decays fast.
