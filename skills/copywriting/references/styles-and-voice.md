# Styles & Voice

Pick one dominant style per asset; layer it under the brand voice.

| Style | Sounds like | Use for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Authoritative** | Confident, data-backed, declarative | Research reports, thought leadership, enterprise | Arrogance; unsupported claims |
| **Conversational** | Plain words, contractions, direct "you" | Blog, email, social, SMB/mid-market | Being too casual for regulated buyers |
| **Technical** | Precise, spec-driven, minimal adjectives | Developer, IT, security audiences, docs-adjacent pages | Jargon walls; lead with the outcome first |
| **Provocative/contrarian** | Challenges a belief ("Your MQLs are lying to you") | Category creation, ads, LinkedIn | Must be backed by a real POV and proof |
| **Executive/brief** | Bottom line up front, bullets, numbers | C-suite emails, one-pagers, board-level content | Losing the emotional "why" |
| **Empathetic/consultative** | Acknowledges pain, asks questions | Nurture, customer success, sensitive industries | Wordiness |
| **Story-driven** | Narrative, a protagonist, before/after | Case studies, founder posts, video | Burying the outcome |

## Voice consistency rules
- Write a "this, not that" list (e.g. "direct, not blunt"; "expert, not academic").
- Keep a banned-words list in the context file.
- Read it aloud. If it doesn't sound like a smart person explaining something to a peer, rewrite it.
