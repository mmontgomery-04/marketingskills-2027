# Headline Formulas

- **Outcome + timeframe:** "Close your books in 3 days, not 3 weeks"
- **Outcome without pain:** "SOC 2 compliance without the spreadsheet chaos"
- **For [audience] who [situation]:** "Payroll for remote teams that hire in 40+ countries"
- **Contrarian:** "Stop buying intent data you can't act on"
- **Number + specificity:** "7 RevOps reports every CRO asks for"
- **Question that qualifies:** "Still reconciling pipeline in spreadsheets?"
- **Category + differentiator:** "The only CRM built for multi-entity finance teams"
- **Social proof:** "How 2,300 security teams cut alert noise by 60%"

Test: would a reader know exactly what they get and for whom? If not, rewrite.
