# Writing Citable Content for LLMs

Write for people first. Google says AI-specific rewrites and "chunking" give no special advantage in its AI features; these rules are simply clear writing that both readers and AI systems can quote accurately.

## Writing rules
- **Answer first.** The first 1–2 sentences under a heading should answer it.
- **Sections that stand up on their own.** Avoid relying on "as mentioned above" for key facts. Don't break content into artificial fragments for bots.
- **Entity clarity.** Name the product, category, and audience explicitly ("Acme is a SOC 2 compliance platform for Series A–C SaaS companies").
- **Specifics beat adjectives.** "Cuts audit prep from 6 weeks to 10 days" beats "dramatically faster".
- **Structured comparisons.** Tables with consistent attributes; "Best for: …" lines.
- **Definitions.** A one-sentence "What is X?" near the top of category pages.
- **Sourced stats.** Original data gets cited far more than recycled data; publish your own.

## Page types that get cited most
1. Comparison and "alternatives" pages
2. Original research and benchmark reports
3. Definitional / glossary pages
4. Step-by-step how-tos with numbered steps
5. Pricing pages with clear plan tables

## Avoid
- Gated content for answers you want cited
- Key content inside images, PDFs-only, tabs, or JS accordions that don't render server-side
