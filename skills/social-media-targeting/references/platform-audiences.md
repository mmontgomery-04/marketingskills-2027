# Platform Audience Options

Targeting options change. Confirm current options in each ad manager before building.

## LinkedIn
- **Professional attributes:** job function, seniority, job title, skills, years of experience, groups, member interests
- **Company attributes:** company name, industry, size, growth rate, follower of a page
- **Matched audiences:** company list upload, contact list upload, website retargeting (Insight Tag), video/lead form/event/document engagement, company page visitors
- **Predictive audiences** (LinkedIn's AI-built lookalike replacement) from a seed of leads or customers
- Tip: job function + seniority is usually broader and more stable than exact titles. Use titles for narrow persona tests.
- Turn off Audience Expansion when precision matters.

## Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- Weak native B2B firmographics. Rely on custom audiences (CRM lists, site visitors, engagers) and lookalikes, or broad targeting with strong creative and Conversions API signals.
- Good for retargeting and for SMB/founder audiences.

## YouTube / Google
- Customer Match, custom segments (based on search terms and URLs), in-market and affinity audiences, website remarketing.

## X
- Follower lookalikes (followers of industry accounts), keyword and conversation targeting, list uploads.

## Reddit
- Subreddit (community) targeting, interest and keyword targeting, custom audiences.
