# Design Principles & Critique Checklist

## Principles
- **Hierarchy:** size, weight, color, and position should make the reading order obvious.
- **Contrast:** important elements stand out from their surroundings.
- **Alignment:** everything lines up to a grid, which makes layouts feel intentional.
- **Proximity:** related items sit together, and unrelated items have space between them.
- **Repetition:** consistent styles across an asset and across a campaign build recognition.
- **Whitespace:** space around elements gives them importance and makes the design easier to scan.

## Data visualization
Pick the chart for the comparison (bar for categories, line for trends over time, a single big number for one key stat). Highlight the one data point that matters, label directly instead of relying on legends, start bar axes at zero, and always cite the source.

## Critique checklist
- [ ] Can someone get the message in under 3 seconds?
- [ ] Is there one clear focal point and one CTA?
- [ ] Does it follow the brand system?
- [ ] Is text readable on a phone, with contrast of at least 4.5:1?
- [ ] Are images licensed and screenshots current?
- [ ] Is the copy final, proofread, and free of AI-isms?
- [ ] Are files exported in the right sizes and formats?
