# Creative Brief Template

```markdown
# Creative Brief: [asset name]
- Goal (what should the viewer do or believe?):
- Audience (persona and awareness stage):
- Placement and format (e.g. LinkedIn single image 1200×1200, A4 one-pager PDF):
- Single key message:
- Supporting points (max 3):
- Proof to show (metric, logo, quote, screenshot):
- CTA:
- Mandatory elements (logo, legal line, URL, QR code):
- Brand references and examples we like (and why):
- What to avoid:
- Copy (final, approved text):
- Variations needed (sizes, A/B angles):
- Deadline and reviewers:
```
Approve the copy before design starts, since late copy changes cause most rework.
