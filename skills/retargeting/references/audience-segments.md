# Audience Segments

| Segment | Definition | Suggested window | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| High intent | Visited pricing, demo, comparison, or "contact" pages without converting | 7–30 days | Hot |
| Product interest | Visited product, feature, integration, or use-case pages | 30–60 days | Warm |
| Proof seekers | Read case studies or customer pages | 30–60 days | Warm |
| Content readers | Read 2+ blog posts or a pillar page | 30–90 days | Cool |
| Video viewers | Watched 50%+ of a product or educational video | 30–90 days | Warm |
| Lead form openers | Opened but didn't submit a platform lead form | 30 days | Warm |
| Event registrants / attendees | Registered or attended a webinar or event | 30–90 days | Warm |
| Trial or signup abandoners | Started signup or trial without activating | 7–14 days | Hot |
| CRM lists | Open opportunities, closed-lost, stalled deals, target accounts | Synced continuously | Varies |

Windows are starting points; adjust to your sales cycle and audience size. Check each platform's minimum audience size before splitting too finely.

## Standard exclusions
Employees, current customers (unless upselling), people who converted on the offer, competitors where identifiable, and job-seeker traffic (careers pages).
