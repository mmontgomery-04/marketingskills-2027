# Platform Setup Notes

Platform features and minimums change. Confirm details in each ad manager.

## Google Ads
Website segments (formerly remarketing lists), Customer Match from CRM lists, YouTube viewer segments, and remarketing layered on search campaigns as an observation or targeting signal. Display and YouTube placements have frequency management controls.

## LinkedIn
Matched audiences from website retargeting (Insight Tag, segmented by URL), video viewers, lead form openers, document ad engagers, event attendees, company page visitors, and contact or company list uploads. Turn off Audience Expansion on retargeting campaigns.

## Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
Custom audiences from the Pixel plus Conversions API, engagement audiences (video, lead form, page or profile), and customer lists. Strong for cheap frequency on warm audiences.

## Microsoft Ads
Website audiences via the UET tag and customer match lists; useful for professional audiences on Bing search.

## Naming convention
`RT_[platform]_[segment]_[window]` (e.g. `RT_LI_pricing_30d`) so reports line up across platforms.
