# Gating & Follow-up

## Gate decision
| Gate it | Leave it ungated |
|---|---|
| Calculators, assessments, templates with real effort | Educational blog-style content |
| Original research (gate the full report, publish key stats openly for PR/AI) | Content you want ranked and cited |
| BOFU assets requested by in-market buyers | Brand awareness pieces |

**Hybrid:** publish an ungated summary and gate the full asset or the editable template.

## Form fields
Email + company is enough for MOFU. Use enrichment (HubSpot Breeze Intelligence, formerly Clearbit; Apollo; ZoomInfo; Clay) instead of long forms. Each extra field costs conversions.

## Follow-up sequence (MOFU magnet)
1. Immediate: deliver the asset + one quick-win tip
2. Day 2: related insight or how a customer used it
3. Day 5: case study
4. Day 9: BOFU offer (assessment, demo, consult)
5. Day 14: breakup / preference check

Route high-fit leads to sales according to revops scoring rules, don't wait for the sequence to end.
