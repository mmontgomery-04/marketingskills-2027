# Lead Magnets & Collateral (MOFU/BOFU)

## Before You Start

1. **Load shared context.** If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first and only ask for what's missing. If it doesn't exist, offer to run the [marketing-context](../marketing-context/SKILL.md) skill (5–10 minutes) or proceed with a few quick questions.
2. **Confirm the job.** Restate the task, the audience, and what "done" looks like in one or two sentences before producing work.

## Step 1: Define the job
- **Stage:** MOFU (educate, earn the email, build trust) or BOFU (help a buyer choose and justify)?
- **Use:** inbound capture, paid social offer, event giveaway, sales leave-behind, or nurture content?
- **Reader:** which buying committee member? (see copywriting's buying-committee table)

## Step 2: Pick the format
Use [formats.md](references/formats.md). Fast rule: the best magnets solve one specific problem right now. A template or calculator beats a 40-page ebook most of the time.

## Step 3: Outline and build
- One-pagers and infographics: use the layouts in [formats.md](references/formats.md).
- Long guides: outline with the content-strategy brief, write with copywriting, add 1 custom visual per major section.
- Always include: a clear title promising the outcome, the brand, a next-step CTA appropriate to the stage, and a date.

## Step 4: Gate or not
Decide with [gating-and-follow-up.md](references/gating-and-follow-up.md). Default: ungate content you want cited and shared, gate high-effort tools and original data, and ask only for fields you'll use.

## Step 5: Launch and follow-up
Landing page (cro), promotion (social, paid-social, email), and a 3–5 email follow-up sequence (email-marketing) that moves toward the BOFU next step.

## Output
Asset brief → full outline or draft → landing-page copy → follow-up sequence outline → success metrics (conversion rate, cost per lead, influenced pipeline).
