# Landing Page Audit

Work through the areas in order. The first rows carry the most weight, so fix a muddled value proposition before polishing buttons. Score each 0–2; anything at 0 is a fix-now item.

| # | Area | Questions | Where it usually breaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Message match** | Does the headline pick up the promise of the ad, email, or link that sent the visitor? | Paid clicks sent to the homepage; the offer in the ad is nowhere on the page |
| 2 | **Clarity (5-second test)** | Can a stranger say what it is, who it's for, and what to do next? | Category never named; internal product names; abstract hero image |
| 3 | **Value proposition** | Outcome-led, specific, differentiated, and in the customer's own words? | A list of features with no result; a clever line that needs explaining; five messages fighting for the top spot |
| 4 | **Headline and subhead** | Does the headline carry the main benefit, and the subhead say who it's for and how it works? | Lines any competitor could use ("The platform for modern teams") |
| 5 | **CTA** | One primary action, on the first screen, saying what happens next? A lighter secondary option for visitors who aren't ready? Repeated after the sections that make the case? | Vague labels; two equal-weight buttons competing; a single CTA at the top of a long page |
| 6 | **Proof** | Logos, metrics, testimonials, ratings, placed beside the CTA and right after the claims they back? | Anonymous quotes; a logo wall with no numbers; all proof parked in the footer |
| 7 | **Objections** | Price, security, integration, effort, switching risk: answered on the page? | Questions sales hears on every call that the page never touches |
| 8 | **Friction** | Form length, required fields, popups, layout shift, an unclear next step? | Asking for phone and company size before any value is shown |
| 9 | **Visual hierarchy** | Does the eye land on the CTA? Can a skimmer get the story from headings alone? Do images show the product or the result? | Walls of text; stock photos; a CTA that blends into the page |
| 10 | **Mobile** | Thumb-friendly CTA, readable text, usable form? | Tables and forms that break on small screens |
| 11 | **Trust** | Security badges, privacy note by the form, real photos, contact details? | No way to reach a human; no security info for IT buyers |
| 12 | **Speed** | LCP under 2.5s on mobile? | Heavy hero video, chat widgets, and tag bloat |

## CTA wording
Name the result or the next step from the visitor's side: "Book a 20-minute demo", "Get the pricing sheet", "Download the 2026 benchmark", "Try it with your own data". Avoid labels that describe the click instead of the payoff: "Submit", "Click here", "Continue".

## Proof that persuades
- **Attributed:** name, title, company, and photo.
- **Specific:** a number or a before/after, not "great product".
- **Matched:** from the same industry, size, or role as the visitor.
- **Placed:** next to the claim it supports and next to the CTA.
- **Real:** never placeholder or invented (see copywriting's Don'ts).

## Answering objections on the page
| Objection | Ways to answer it |
|---|---|
| "Is it worth the price?" | Visible pricing, an ROI calculator, a payback example from a real customer |
| "Will it work for us?" | Industry or use-case sections; proof from a customer in the same segment |
| "How much work is setup?" | Implementation steps with timelines, an integrations list, who does what |
| "What if we regret this?" | Trial or pilot terms, cancellation terms, migration help |
| "Is it secure?" | Security page or trust center, certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001), data handling summary |

Good homes for these answers: an FAQ near the CTA, a comparison table, and a short "how it works" sequence.
