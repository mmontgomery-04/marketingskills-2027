# Launch Tiers

| Tier | What | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | New product, major platform change, category move | Positioning refresh, press/analyst briefings, launch event or webinar, website update, paid campaign, customer email, sales training, battlecard updates |
| **Tier 2** | Significant feature for a key segment | Blog, customer email, in-app announcement, social, sales enablement note, targeted ads |
| **Tier 3** | Improvements and small features | Changelog, in-app tooltip, help docs, social mention |

## Launch checklist (Tier 1)
- [ ] Positioning and messaging approved
- [ ] Pricing/packaging decided
- [ ] Website, docs, and demo environment updated
- [ ] Sales deck, battlecard, FAQ, and objection handling ready; sales trained
- [ ] PR/analyst plan (digital-pr), customer advocates lined up
- [ ] Campaign assets: email, social, paid, webinar
- [ ] Success metrics and dashboard (analytics)
- [ ] Post-launch retro at 30 days
