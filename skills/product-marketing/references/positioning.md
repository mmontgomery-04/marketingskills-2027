# Positioning

## Positioning statement
> For [target customer] who [need/pain], [product] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [primary alternative], we [key differentiator].

## Differentiation tests
- **Unique:** could a competitor say the same sentence? Then it's table stakes.
- **Valuable:** does the buyer care, measured by money, time, or risk?
- **Provable:** do we have a metric, logo, or demo moment that shows it?

## Category choices
- **Existing category:** easiest to understand; you compete on differentiators.
- **Subcategory:** "X for Y" (e.g. "CRM for multi-entity finance teams"); owns a niche.
- **New category:** expensive and slow; only when the alternative frame genuinely doesn't fit.
