# Briefs & Contracts

## Creator brief
- About us (2 lines) and why we chose them
- Goal and audience
- Key message (one) + 2–3 supporting facts with proof
- Must-include: disclosure, link/code, product name spelling
- Must-avoid: claims we can't support, competitor mentions
- Creative freedom: format and voice are theirs
- Deliverables, dates, review process (one round of factual review)

## Contract essentials
Deliverables and dates · payment terms · usage rights (organic only vs paid amplification, duration) · exclusivity window (category, duration) · disclosure requirements · approval process · cancellation and kill fees · performance reporting.

## Disclosure
In the US, the FTC requires clear and conspicuous disclosure of material connections ("#ad", "Sponsored", "Paid partnership" labels at the start of the post, not buried). Other regions have similar rules (e.g. UK ASA/CMA). Employees promoting their employer should disclose the relationship too.
