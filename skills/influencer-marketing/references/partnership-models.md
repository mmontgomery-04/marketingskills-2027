# Partnership Models

| Model | Description | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsored post / series | Paid LinkedIn or YouTube content | Launch awareness |
| Newsletter sponsorship | Ad slot or dedicated send | Targeted reach to niche audiences |
| Podcast sponsorship / guesting | Host-read ad or appearance | Trust and depth |
| Co-created content | Webinar, report, or template with the creator | Lead gen + credibility |
| Ambassador program | Ongoing relationship, perks, early access | Sustained advocacy |
| Affiliate / referral | Commission on revenue | PLG and lower-ACV products |
| Creator-led ads (thought leader ads) | Boosting a creator's or employee's post on LinkedIn | Higher engagement than brand ads |
| Employee advocacy | Execs and SMEs posting regularly | Always-on, lowest cost |
| Customer advocacy | Customers as speakers and reviewers | BOFU proof |
