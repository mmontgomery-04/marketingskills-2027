# Creative Strategy & Testing

## Angles to test
Pain (in customer words) · outcome/metric · contrarian POV · social proof (customer quote) · how-it-works demo · offer-led · founder/expert face · comparison vs status quo.

## Principles
- Hook in the first line of copy and first 3 seconds of video.
- Native look beats polished brand ads; faces and real screenshots perform well.
- One message per ad. Big, readable text on images; design for mobile.
- Subtitles on all video.

## Testing method
- Test one variable at a time where budgets allow (angle first, then format, then details).
- Give each test enough impressions/clicks to read; don't call it after a day.
- Keep a creative log: angle, format, hook, result, learning.
- Refresh top performers every 4–8 weeks with new hooks on proven angles.
