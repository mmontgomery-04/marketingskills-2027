# Meta, YouTube, X & Reddit

## Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- Best for retargeting, SMB and founder audiences, and cheap reach for video.
- Use Conversions API + Pixel; optimize to the deepest event with enough volume (send CRM qualified-lead events back).
- Broad targeting with strong creative often works; let creative do the targeting.
- Lead forms: use "higher intent" form type and add qualifying questions.

## YouTube
- Awareness and education at low CPMs; retarget viewers on LinkedIn/Meta.
- Use custom segments built from competitor URLs and category search terms.

## X
- Tech, developer, and VC audiences; follower lookalikes and keyword targeting.

## Reddit
- Subreddit targeting for practitioners; native, transparent, non-salesy creative; keep comments on and respond.
