# LinkedIn Ads

## Formats
- **Single image:** reliable workhorse
- **Document ads:** carousels/PDFs; can gate inside the ad with a lead form
- **Video:** awareness and retargeting pools
- **Thought Leader ads:** boost posts from employees or creators; often higher engagement and lower cost than brand ads
- **Conversation ads:** for high-value invites (events, exec offers); use sparingly
- **Lead Gen Forms:** pre-filled, high conversion, lower intent than site forms; keep to 3–5 fields and sync to the CRM instantly

## Setup checklist
- [ ] Insight Tag and Conversions API configured; conversions defined
- [ ] Audience Expansion off for precise B2B targeting
- [ ] LinkedIn Audience Network off initially (turn on only after testing placement quality)
- [ ] Exclusions: customers, competitors, employees, recent converters
- [ ] 3–5 ads per campaign with distinct angles
- [ ] Frequency monitored; refresh creative when performance decays

## Bidding
Start with maximum delivery (automated) to learn costs, then move to cost caps or manual bids if CPCs run high. LinkedIn CPMs and CPCs are high relative to other platforms. Judge by pipeline value, not cost per click.
