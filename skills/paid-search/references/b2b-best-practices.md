# B2B Paid Search Best Practices

- **Optimize to pipeline, not leads.** Import offline conversions (enhanced conversions for leads or CRM integrations with HubSpot/Salesforce). Form-fill optimization finds people who fill forms, not buyers.
- **Qualify in the ad.** Mention price ("from $500/mo"), audience ("for teams of 50+"), or "enterprise" to deter bad clicks.
- **Offer ladder:** high intent → demo/trial; medium → assessment, calculator, or comparison guide; low → content.
- **Competitor bidding:** don't use competitor trademarks in ad text where restricted; lead with your differentiators; expect lower CTR and higher CPC.
- **Microsoft Ads:** import Google campaigns; often cheaper CPCs and a desktop-heavy professional audience. Check current audience targeting options in the platform.
- **Budget pacing:** watch impression share lost to budget; concentrate budget on fewer, better campaigns instead of spreading thin.
- **Brand defense:** keep brand campaigns on unless tests show organic captures the same conversions; test with geo holdouts.
