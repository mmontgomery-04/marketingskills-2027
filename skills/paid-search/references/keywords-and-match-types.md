# Keywords & Match Types

| Match type | Syntax | Behavior | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact | `[keyword]` | Same meaning/intent as the keyword (includes close variants) | Core high-intent and brand terms |
| Phrase | `"keyword"` | Includes the meaning of the keyword | Mid-tail discovery with control |
| Broad | `keyword` | Related searches, uses signals | Only with smart bidding, strong conversion data, and tight negatives |

## Negative keyword lists (starter)
- Jobs: jobs, careers, salary, hiring, internship
- Education: course, certification, tutorial, definition, pdf, free template (if you don't offer one)
- Wrong fit: free, open source, cheap, personal, for students (adjust to your offer)
- Support: login, sign in, customer service, phone number (on non-brand)

Review the search terms report weekly for the first month, then every two weeks.
