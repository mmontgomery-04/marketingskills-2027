# Title & Meta Formulas

## Title tag patterns
- `[Keyword]: [Process], [Examples] & [Benefit]`
- `How to [Keyword] in [N] Steps ([Year])`
- `[Keyword] vs [Alt]: Key Differences, Pros & Cons`
- `What Is [Keyword]? Definition, Examples & Best Practices`
- `[N] Best [Keyword] for [Audience] in [Year]`

## Meta description patterns (155–160 chars)
- `Learn how to [outcome] with [keyword]. This guide covers [A], [B], and [C], plus examples you can use today.`
- `[Keyword] is [one-line definition]. Find out how it works, when to use it, and how [audience] can [outcome].`

## QA
- Unique per page; no duplicate titles across the site.
- Don't truncate the keyword: check a SERP preview at ~580px.
- Match search intent: a "how to" query wants a process, not a product pitch.
