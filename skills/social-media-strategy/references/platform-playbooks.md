# Platform Playbooks (B2B)

Platform algorithms change often. Re-check native guidance and your own data each quarter instead of trusting fixed rules.

| Platform | Best for | Formats that tend to work | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Most B2B buyers, exec reach, hiring | Text + image, document/carousel posts, short native video, polls (sparingly), newsletters | Personal profiles outperform pages. Dwell time and meaningful comments matter. Test link-in-post vs link-in-comment on your own account rather than assuming. |
| **YouTube** | Search-driven education, demos, webinars, long-lived content | How-tos, teardowns, customer stories, Shorts for discovery | Second-largest search engine; videos also surface in Google and AI answers. |
| **X** | Developer, VC, tech, and media audiences | Threads, quick takes, live commentary | Strong for real-time and newsjacking; weaker for most non-tech B2B. |
| **Reddit** | Practitioner research; heavily cited by AI search | Genuine answers in relevant subreddits, AMAs | Disclose affiliation; self-promotion gets banned. |
| **Instagram / TikTok** | Employer brand, SMB and creator-adjacent B2B | Short video, behind the scenes, education | Test only if the ICP is there. |
| **Communities (Slack, Discord, niche forums)** | Deep trust with practitioners | Helpful answers, events, resources | Give far more than you take. |
