# Content Pillars & Cadence

## Example pillars
1. **Expertise:** how-tos, frameworks, teardowns (40%)
2. **Point of view:** contrarian or forward-looking takes (20%)
3. **Proof:** customer stories, results, behind-the-scenes builds (20%)
4. **Human:** lessons, culture, team, the founder's journey (15%)
5. **Promotion:** launches, events, offers (5–10%)

## Starting cadence
| Voice | Platform | Posts/week |
|---|---|---|
| Founder/CEO | LinkedIn | 3–5 |
| SMEs (2–3) | LinkedIn | 1–2 each |
| Company page | LinkedIn | 3–5 (amplify people, share proof, events) |
| Brand | Secondary channel | 1–3 |

## Post structure (LinkedIn)
Hook line (specific, no clickbait) → short lines with white space → one insight, story, or framework → takeaway → question or soft CTA.

## Repurposing loop
One webinar → recap blog → 3–5 clips → carousel → 5 text posts → newsletter item.
