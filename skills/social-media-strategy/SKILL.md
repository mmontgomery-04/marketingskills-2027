# Social Media Strategy (Organic, B2B)

## Before You Start

1. **Load shared context.** If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first and only ask for what's missing. If it doesn't exist, offer to run the [marketing-context](../marketing-context/SKILL.md) skill (5–10 minutes) or proceed with a few quick questions.
2. **Confirm the job.** Restate the task, the audience, and what "done" looks like in one or two sentences before producing work.

## Workflow

### 1. Goal per channel
Pick one primary job for each platform: reach new ICP buyers, build founder/brand authority, drive demand to content/events, recruit, or support customers. A channel without a job gets cut.

### 2. Choose platforms
Go where the ICP actually spends attention. Details in [platform-playbooks.md](references/platform-playbooks.md). For most B2B companies: LinkedIn first, plus one secondary channel (YouTube, X, Reddit, or a niche community).

### 3. People over logos
Personal profiles (founders, executives, SMEs, employees) usually earn far more reach and trust than company pages. Build the plan around 2–5 voices, with the brand page amplifying them.

### 4. Pillars and formats
Define 3–5 content pillars and a format mix with [pillars-and-cadence.md](references/pillars-and-cadence.md). Lean on the ICP's pains and the brand's real point of view.

### 5. Cadence and workflow
Consistency beats volume. Build a weekly rhythm, a content bank, approval rules (light, fast), and a repurposing loop from blogs, webinars, and research.

### 6. Engagement
Spend as much time commenting on ICP and creator posts as posting. Reply to every comment in the first hour.

### 7. Measure and iterate
Hand KPIs to social-media-reporting. Review monthly; double down on the formats and topics that drive profile visits, followers in ICP, and conversations.

## Output
Strategy one-pager (goals, platforms, voices, pillars, formats, cadence, KPIs) and a 4-week content calendar.

## Avoid
- Engagement pods, follow-for-follow, and bought followers (they hurt reach quality and credibility)
- Generic AI-written posts with no point of view
- Posting the same copy on every platform unchanged
