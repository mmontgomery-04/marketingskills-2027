# CRM & MAP Hygiene

- Required fields at each stage (e.g. opp can't move to Stage 2 without amount, close date, primary contact).
- Picklists for lead source, industry, and loss reason; no free text.
- Lead source vs original source vs latest source: define all three and never overwrite original source.
- UTM capture into hidden form fields → CRM fields (with analytics).
- Dedupe rules on email domain + company; merge weekly.
- Contact-to-account matching (lead-to-account) for B2B routing.
- Data decay: re-verify contacts every 6–12 months.
- Document everything: a field dictionary everyone can read.
