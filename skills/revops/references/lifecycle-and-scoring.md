# Lifecycle Stages & Lead Scoring

| Stage | Entry criteria | Owner | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscriber | Opted in to content | Marketing | Shows engagement |
| Lead | Known contact, some engagement | Marketing | Hits MQL threshold |
| MQL | Fit + engagement ≥ threshold, or hand-raise (demo/contact) | Marketing → SDR | Accepted or rejected (reason required) |
| SAL / SQL | SDR qualified (need, authority, timing) | SDR | Meeting held → Opp |
| Opportunity (SQO) | AE accepts, discovery done | AE | Closed won/lost |
| Customer | Closed won | CS | Expansion / churn |

## Scoring model example
**Fit (0–50):** title/seniority 15 · industry 10 · company size 15 · tech 10
**Engagement (0–50):** demo request = instant MQL · pricing page visit 10 · webinar attended 10 · content download 5 · email click 2 · decay −5 per 30 days inactive
**MQL:** fit ≥ 30 AND engagement ≥ 25, or any hand-raise.

Calibrate: compare MQL→SQO conversion by score band each quarter.
