# Editorial Operations

## Cadence
Consistency beats volume. Two great posts a week out-perform five thin ones. Reserve ~30% of capacity for refreshes.

## Brief template
`working title | primary/secondary keywords | intent | audience | funnel stage | angle/POV | outline (H2s) | SME to interview | internal links | CTA | due date`

## Workflow
Brief → SME interview → draft → on-page-seo pass → edit (voice, accuracy) → design/visuals → publish → distribute → measure at 30/90 days.

## Distribution (every piece)
- 3–5 social posts (founder + brand) via social-media-strategy
- Newsletter feature via email-marketing
- Sales enablement snippet for relevant deals
- Repurpose: carousel, short video, LinkedIn document post
