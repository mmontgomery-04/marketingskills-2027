# Funnel Mapping for B2B Content

| Stage | Searcher mindset | Formats | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU | "I have a problem" | how-tos, definitions, trend pieces, research | "What is revenue operations?" |
| MOFU | "How do others solve it?" | guides, frameworks, templates, webinars, case studies, lead magnets | "RevOps tech stack template" |
| BOFU | "Which vendor?" | comparisons, alternatives, pricing, ROI calculators, integration and use-case pages, demos | "[Brand] vs [Competitor]" |

BOFU pages are typically the highest-converting organic pages per visit. Hand MOFU assets to lead-magnets and BOFU pages to copywriting + cro.
