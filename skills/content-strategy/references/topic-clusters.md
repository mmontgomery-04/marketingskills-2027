# Topic Clusters

- **Pillar page:** broad, 2.5K–3K words, targets the head term ("lead scoring").
- **Spokes:** specific subtopics ("lead scoring models", "predictive lead scoring", "lead scoring in HubSpot"), each linking back to the pillar with descriptive anchor text.
- The pillar links out to every spoke; spokes cross-link where relevant.
- One primary keyword per URL. If two URLs target the same intent, consolidate them to avoid cannibalization.
- Coverage beats volume: owning 20 tightly related pages tends to outrank one big page on a broad term.
