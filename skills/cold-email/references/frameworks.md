# Cold Email Frameworks

**Trigger → Problem → Proof → Ask**
> Saw [company] just opened an office in Austin. Teams expanding into new states usually hit [problem] around month two. We helped [similar company] [result]. Worth a quick look?

**Observation → Insight → Question**
> Noticed your pricing page dropped the free tier. When [peer] did the same, trial volume fell ~30% until they [insight]. Is that on your radar?

**PAS (short)**
> [Persona]s at [segment] tell us [pain in their words]. It usually costs [consequence]. [Product] fixes that by [mechanism]. Open to seeing how?

**Give first (value-led)**
> Put together a 3-point teardown of your onboarding emails. Want me to send it over?

## Follow-up angles (each adds something new)
1. Case study from a lookalike company
2. A relevant insight, stat, or short resource
3. A different persona's angle or a different pain
4. Social proof from their industry
5. Breakup: "Should I close the loop?"

## Subject lines
Lowercase, 2–4 words, no clickbait: `{{company}} + hiring`, `austin expansion`, `quick idea for {{team}}`.
