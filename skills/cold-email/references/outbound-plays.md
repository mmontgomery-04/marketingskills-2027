# Outbound Plays

A play is the offer behind the sequence. Pick the play first; the copy follows from it.

| Play | Best fit | How it runs |
|---|---|---|
| **Do a slice of the work** | Agencies, consultants, services | Do a small, real piece of the job for them (three fixes to their onboarding emails, a quick audit of their ad account) and offer to send it. The work is the proof |
| **Usage-led** | Self-serve or freemium SaaS | Reach out at accounts where people already use the free product. Lead with what that team is doing, then offer help to scale it. Only use data your terms and privacy policy allow, and never sound like you're watching them |
| **Free sample** | Data, research, lead lists, content services | Send a small sample of the output that fits their problem (ten target accounts, a one-page benchmark for their segment) |
| **Signal-led** | Most B2B products | Time the email to a buying signal from [prospecting](../../prospecting/SKILL.md): a new leader, funding, a hiring spike, a stack change. The signal is the opener |
| **Founder-led** | Early-stage companies | The founder writes from their own inbox to a short Tier 1 list. Why they built it and what they've learned from customers carries more weight than any template |

## Choosing
- Can you show value before the call? Use a slice of the work or a free sample.
- Do target accounts already touch your product? Go usage-led.
- Is timing the main thing? Go signal-led.
- Under roughly 20 customers? The founder should be sending.
