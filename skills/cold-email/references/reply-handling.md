# Reply Handling

Sequences exist to start conversations. Most of the value is lost when replies sit for a day.

## Sort every reply
| Reply type | Looks like | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Interested | "Sure, tell me more" / "Let's talk" | Answer fast. Offer two specific times in their time zone plus a booking link |
| Question | "How does pricing work?" / "Do you integrate with X?" | Answer in two or three lines, then make the ask again |
| Objection | "We already use X" / "No budget" | Respond once, briefly (see below). Don't argue |
| Timing | "Not now, try Q3" | Thank them, stop the sequence, set a dated follow-up task |
| Referral | "Talk to Priya, she owns this" | Thank them, email the new contact and mention who sent you (ask first if it's unclear they're happy to be named) |
| Out of office | Auto-reply with a return date | Pause, then resume a day or two after they're back |
| Opt-out | "Remove me" / "Stop" | Remove right away, across every tool and channel. No reply needed |
| Hostile | Angry or rude | Remove, don't reply, and check whether targeting went wrong |

## Speed
Answer interested replies within the hour during business hours; minutes is better. Interest fades quickly, and the prospect may have replied to a competitor the same morning.

## Short objection answers
- **"We already use X."** Ask what they'd change about it if they could, or share one line on why a similar team switched. Then let it go.
- **"No budget."** Ask when they plan next year's budget and offer to check back then.
- **"Send me some info."** Ask what they'd want to see, then send one link, not a deck.
- **"Not interested."** Thank them and stop. Don't send a "just in case" follow-up.

## What an AI agent may do on its own
| Can act alone | Needs a human to approve or send |
|---|---|
| Classify replies and log them in the CRM | Any reply to an interested prospect or a question |
| Pause or stop sequences across channels | Objection responses |
| Process opt-outs immediately | Pricing, discounts, contract or legal terms |
| Reschedule after out-of-office replies | Hostile replies and complaints |
| Draft responses for review | Anything that commits the company to something |
