# Timelines & RACI

## Example: Tier 1 launch, planned back from launch day (L)
| When | Milestone |
|---|---|
| L–8 weeks | Brief approved, positioning and messaging locked |
| L–6 weeks | Copy drafts for web, email, ads, sales enablement |
| L–5 weeks | Copy approved; design and build start |
| L–3 weeks | Assets in review; tracking plan implemented; sales training scheduled |
| L–2 weeks | Final approvals, legal review, QA of pages and tracking |
| L–1 week | Scheduled sends and posts loaded, ads in review, internal launch brief shared |
| L | Launch; monitor live |
| L+1 week | Early results check and fixes |
| L+30 days | Retro against success metrics |

## RACI
| Task | Responsible | Accountable | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Messaging | PMM | Head of Marketing | Product, Sales | Company |
| Landing page | Web | PMM | Design, SEO | Sales |
| Paid campaign | Demand gen | Head of Demand Gen | PMM, Design | Sales |

One "A" per task. Keep "C" small so feedback rounds stay short.
