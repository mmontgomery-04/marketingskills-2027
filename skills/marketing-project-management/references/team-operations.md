# Team Operations

## Intake
One form for all requests: what's needed, why, audience, deadline, and the business goal it supports. Requests outside the form go back to the form.

## Prioritization
Score requests on impact (pipeline or strategic value), effort, and urgency, and review the queue weekly with stakeholders. Say no or "not this sprint" to low-impact work explicitly, with the reason.

## Sprints or Kanban
- **Sprints (2 weeks):** planning, a short daily or async check-in, a demo of shipped work, and a retro. Good for teams with mixed project and ad-hoc work.
- **Kanban:** columns such as Backlog → Brief → In progress → Review → Approved → Scheduled → Live, with work-in-progress limits per column. Good for content and design teams with steady flow.

## Tool setup (Asana, Monday.com, ClickUp, Jira, Notion)
Templates for recurring work (blog post, webinar, launch, campaign), custom fields for channel, campaign, owner, and status, due dates on every task, and a dashboard of upcoming launches and overdue items.

## Status report
Done this week · In progress · At risk (reason and fix) · Decisions needed · Next week.
