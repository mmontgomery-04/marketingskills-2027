# Digital PR

## Before You Start

1. **Load shared context.** If `.agents/marketing-context.md` exists, read it first and only ask for what's missing. If it doesn't exist, offer to run the [marketing-context](../marketing-context/SKILL.md) skill (5–10 minutes) or proceed with a few quick questions.
2. **Confirm the job.** Restate the task, the audience, and what "done" looks like in one or two sentences before producing work.

## Why it matters now
Digital PR builds authority three ways: editorial backlinks (SEO), brand mentions on sources LLMs cite (ai-search), and credibility for buyers. Plan campaigns for all three.

## Campaign types
Pick from [campaign-types.md](references/campaign-types.md):
1. **Original data / research** (highest ROI, most reusable)
2. **Reactive / newsjacking**: expert commentary on breaking industry news within hours
3. **Expert sourcing**: answering journalist requests on Qwoted, Featured, Source of Sources, or #journorequest
4. **Thought leadership**: bylines, contributed articles, podcast guesting
5. **Announcements**: funding, launches, partnerships (press release + targeted pitches)

## Workflow
1. **Angle:** what's genuinely new, surprising, or useful to a journalist's audience? "We launched a feature" usually isn't news; "60% of CFOs plan to cut tools in 2027" is.
2. **Targets:** 20–50 relevant journalists and publications per campaign, chosen by what they've covered recently. Personalize.
3. **Pitch:** use [pitching.md](references/pitching.md).
4. **Assets:** data summary, charts, expert quotes, spokesperson bio and headshot, landing page with methodology.
5. **Follow-up:** one follow-up after 3–5 days with a new angle or asset, then stop.
6. **Measure:** coverage, referring domains (DR/DA), brand mentions, AI citations, referral traffic, and assisted pipeline.

## Rules
- Never fabricate data, quotes, or credentials.
- Respect embargoes. Correct errors quickly.
