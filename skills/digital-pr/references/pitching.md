# Pitching Journalists

## Pitch email (≤150 words)
```
Subject: [Specific, newsy hook, e.g. "New data: 62% of IT leaders delayed AI rollouts over security"]

Hi [Name],

Your piece on [their recent article] made me think you'd find this useful: [one-sentence finding].

A few more data points:
• [stat]
• [stat]

[Spokesperson, title] can comment on [angle]. Full data and charts: [link].

Happy to send anything else.
[Name]
```

## Rules
- Personalize to their beat and recent work; never mass-blast.
- Put the news in the subject line.
- No attachments on first contact; link to assets.
- Exclusives can land bigger outlets; offer one at a time.
- Track every pitch: journalist, outlet, date, angle, response, coverage link.
