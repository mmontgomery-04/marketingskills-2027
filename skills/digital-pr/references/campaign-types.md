# Digital PR Campaign Types

| Type | Effort | Speed | Link/mention yield | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original survey (n=300+) | High | 6–10 weeks | High, compounding | Use a panel (Pollfish, Censuswide); publish methodology |
| Proprietary product data | Med | 3–6 weeks | High | Anonymize and aggregate; get legal sign-off |
| Data analysis of public sources | Med | 2–4 weeks | Med–High | BLS, Census, SEC filings, public APIs |
| Newsjacking | Low | Hours | Med | Speed matters; prepare spokespeople in advance |
| Expert sourcing | Low | Daily habit | Med | Answer fast, specific, quotable (≤150 words) |
| Bylines / contributed | Med | 2–6 weeks | Med | Pitch the idea before writing |
| Podcast guesting | Low–Med | Ongoing | Med (+ audience) | Pitch a specific episode topic |

## Research report structure
Headline stat → 5–8 key findings with charts → methodology → embeddable graphics → expert commentary → about. Publish the key stats ungated; gate the full report if needed (lead-magnets).
