# Review Generation Program

## Where reviews matter
G2, Capterra (and the Gartner Digital Markets sites), TrustRadius, and Gartner Peer Insights for enterprise. Prioritize the one or two sites your buyers and competitors use most, and check which ones AI assistants cite for your category.

## How to ask
- Ask at happy moments: after a positive support ticket, a high NPS score, a renewal, or a feature launch customers asked for.
- Use in-app prompts, CSM outreach, and email with a direct link to the review form.
- Ask for honest reviews. Don't steer toward positive ones or ask only happy customers through gated flows if the platform prohibits it.
- Follow each platform's incentive rules (several allow small gift cards for any honest review, given regardless of rating).

## Respond to reviews
Reply to every review, including negative ones, within a few days. A calm, specific response to criticism builds trust with readers.

## Use reviews
Embed badges and quotes (with platform permission), use ratings in ads where platform terms allow, and mine reviews for messaging language and objections.
