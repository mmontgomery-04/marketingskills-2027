# References, Advisory Boards & Co-Marketing

## Reference program
Keep a list of reference-ready customers tagged by industry, size, use case, competitor replaced, and region. Limit each customer to a set number of calls per quarter, track every request, and thank them after each call.

## Customer advisory board (CAB)
8–15 customers who represent your ICP. Meet two to four times a year (one in person if possible). Share roadmap direction, ask for honest input, and close the loop on what you changed because of their feedback.

## Co-marketing and speaking
Joint webinars, conference talks, podcast appearances, guest blog posts, and co-branded research. Make it easy: you do the prep, they bring the story.

## Measuring advocacy
Number of active advocates, reviews per quarter and average rating, case studies published, references fulfilled, deals where a reference or case study was used, and win rate in those deals compared with others.
