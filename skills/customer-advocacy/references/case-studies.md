# Case Studies

## Interview questions (30 minutes)
1. What was happening before you looked for a solution? What did it cost you?
2. What else did you consider, and why did you choose us?
3. What was rollout like? How long until you saw value?
4. What results have you seen? (Push for numbers: time saved, revenue, cost, risk reduced.)
5. What would you tell a peer considering this?

Record with permission so quotes are exact.

## Structure
Customer snapshot (industry, size, use case) → the challenge → why they chose you → implementation → results with numbers → a quote → CTA. Put the headline result in the title, e.g. "How [Customer] cut month-end close from 10 days to 4".

## Formats
Full web case study, one-page PDF for sales, 60–90 second video, a short "customer story" social post, and quote cards for ads. One interview should feed all of them.
