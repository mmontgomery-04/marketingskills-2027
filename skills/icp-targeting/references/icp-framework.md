## Trigger events (timing signals)
New executive in the buying role · funding round · hiring surge for a related team · new regulation · M&A · tech stack change · expansion into new markets · poor public reviews of an incumbent.

## Negative ICP
Who churns, stalls, or costs too much to serve. Example: "<50 employees: no admin to own rollout; churn 3× average."

## ICP statement
> We win with [industry] companies of [size] that [situation/trigger], use [tech], and need [outcome] because [pain].
