# Account Tiering & Fit Scoring

## Fit score (0–100)
| Attribute | Weight | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| Industry match | 25 | core 25 · adjacent 10 · other 0 |
| Employee band | 20 | sweet spot 20 · edge 10 |
| Tech stack | 20 | must-have present 20 |
| Region | 10 | |
| Trigger present | 25 | recent (≤90d) 25 · older 10 |

## Tiers
- **Tier 1 (score 80+, ~50–100 accounts):** 1:1 ABM, custom content, exec outreach, events
- **Tier 2 (60–79):** 1:few by cluster, tailored sequences and ads
- **Tier 3 (40–59):** programmatic ads and automated nurture
- **Below 40:** don't spend sales time

Recalibrate quarterly against actual win rates by tier.
